# Megan's Technical Writing Portfolio
This site serves as my portfolio for public available technical writing projects.

While some of documents shown here are focused on customer service roles and tasks, most of my technical experience is with engineering documentation and product manuals; the technical documents I have experience with are proprietary and unavailable for public viewing. The provided documents showcase my ability to create thorough training materials for colleagues to reference in their daily tasks.

In my portfolio is also the rules and gameplay guide that I created for a personal project: a mash-up of Dungeons & Dragons and Clue. I began working on this project in college and did a test run with friends to refine the gameplay before creating a finalized guide. While I never finished the project in its entirety, I continue to hold a special place in my heart for this homemade version of my 2 favorite tabletop games.

Thank you for taking the time to look at my page!
